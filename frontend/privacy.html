Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Kitchen Analytics ("we", "us", "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our cloud kitchen analytics platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account information (name, email, password hash)
  • Business information (kitchen name, city, address)
  • Sales data you upload (orders, items, customer phone numbers)
  • Payment information (processed by Razorpay; we never store card data)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide analytics, dashboards, and business insights
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send transactional emails (password reset, payment receipts)
  • Improve our platform and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases hosted on Supabase (Mumbai region). We use SSL/TLS for all data transfers, hash passwords with industry-standard algorithms, and restrict access to authorized personnel only.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing
  • Supabase — for database hosting
  • Render.com — for API hosting
  • Cloudflare — for frontend hosting and CDN

Each provider is contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the stated purpose.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in CSV format
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

6. Cookies

We use minimal browser storage (localStorage) to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. On account deletion, all your data (orders, items, customers) is permanently removed within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via email and a dashboard banner. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
